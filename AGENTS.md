# Rheobase OS

Rheobase is a YouTube explainer channel. This one workspace does all of the work: ideas, research, script, Owen's voiceover, the animation, sound, the final video, the title and the thumbnail. There is no separate Production team anymore.

The creator's latest instruction always wins over anything written here.

## What we make

Original science and technology explainers in the spirit of **Cleo Abram (Huge If True)** and **Vsauce**, without copying either one. Every picture is drawn by our own code in a hand-made ink and paper style, every scene flows into the next through a connected zoom or morph, and Owen narrates. Full details:

- `memory/channel.md`: who the channel is for, the Cleo and Vsauce blend, and the monetization situation.
- `memory/style.md`: the visual and sound rules. Read it before designing or building anything.
- `memory/voice.md`: how Owen's voiceover is made.
- `memory/videos.md`: every episode and its current state.
- `memory/reference-study.md` and `outputs/`: the kept reference studies. Open one only when it answers a specific question.

## How an episode gets made

Each episode lives in `episodes/YYYY-MM-DD-slug/`. Follow these in order:

1. `workflows/idea.md`: find and pitch the idea. **The creator picks the topic.**
2. `workflows/script.md`: research, write, fact-check every claim. **The creator approves the exact script.**
3. `workflows/voice.md` and `workflows/build.md`: record Owen, then build the first 60 seconds of the real episode. **The creator approves the look.** Then build the rest.
4. `workflows/publish.md`: full-cut review, title and thumbnail. **The creator approves and uploads.**

Those four bold points are the only times to stop and wait. Between them, keep working and make the ordinary decisions yourself.

## Rules that are never broken

1. **Original or nothing.** The channel was refused monetization for reused content. We never use other creators' footage, clips, music, scripts or structures. Every frame is drawn by our code, every word is ours, and Owen's voice is ours.
2. **True.** Every factual claim in a script is checked against a source we actually opened. If it can't be supported, it's cut. Visuals must not imply anything false either.
3. **Watch it before calling it done.** Judge a scene by looking at the real rendered output, not by the code or a plan. Say exactly what was and wasn't checked. Only the creator can listen to the audio.
4. **Know what you're building before you build.** Write the scene chain (what each scene shows and what it turns into) before any code.
5. **No paid services or spending** without the creator's explicit yes.
6. **No em dashes** in anything we write.
7. **Never delete anything without asking.** Use the Recycle Bin, never permanent deletion.
8. **YouTube account actions belong to the creator:** uploading, deleting or editing videos, and reapplying for monetization.

## Keeping records

Keep it light. Each episode has one `state.md` saying where it stands and what happens next. Update `memory/videos.md` when an episode changes stage. Put a lasting creator decision in the right `memory/` file once. Log changes to this OS as one line in `memory/os-log.md`. Don't write reports about routine work.

The old two-team OS is in `archive/2026-11-05-pre-rebuild/`. It's history, not instructions.
