# Publish: final cut, title, thumbnail, release

## Final cut

Before giving the creator the full cut, confirm that:
- the export is at least 10 minutes, with no padding, held frames or slowed speech;
- the script matches the approved version and the fact-check record;
- there's no on-screen text, every transition is connected, and your own review is done (see `build.md`);
- everything in it is original: our code, our words, Owen's voice, our score.

**The creator watches the full cut and approves or gives notes.** Fix the notes, then show it again.

## Title and thumbnail

- Write 3 to 5 honest titles that promise what the video actually delivers. Lean into the question or the surprising destination of the chain. Don't copy Cleo's or Vsauce's title patterns.
- Make 2 or 3 thumbnails rendered from the episode's scene code (see `memory/style.md`). Check that each one is readable at phone size.
- Recommend one title and thumbnail pair. The creator chooses.

## Release package

Save `publish.md` in the episode folder with:
- the title options and the pick;
- the description: a short hook, the sources list taken from the fact-check, and a note that the narration is a synthetic voice;
- an .srt subtitle file made from the narration timing;
- notes on YouTube's current disclosure rules, checked at the time of release rather than from memory;
- the final MP4 path and the thumbnail files.

**The creator uploads and publishes.** Claude never acts on the YouTube account.

## After release

When the creator shares real numbers or comments (views, average view duration, retention dips, CTR, viewer questions), write a short `retro.md` covering what worked, what to change, and ideas from viewer questions. Watch hours toward the 4,000 goal are tracked in `memory/channel.md`.
